# Antitrust Policy

The Weight Custody Manifest is an open specification project hosted under the agentrust-io organization, targeting a standards path through IETF SCITT and CoSAI. Participation is subject to antitrust and competition laws.

## Prohibited topics

The following must not be discussed in any project forum, issue, PR, meeting, or chat channel:

- Prices, pricing strategies, or cost structures of any product or service
- Market allocation, geographic territories, or customer segmentation
- Bids, contracts, or terms with any customer or competitor
- Whether to deal with, or boycott, any company or individual
- Production volumes, capacity plans, or sales forecasts
- Any other competitively sensitive business information

## What is appropriate

Project discussion is limited to technical matters: specification design, cryptographic protocols, attestation and custody semantics, SDK implementation, test coverage, and interoperability. Commercial decisions are for each organization to make independently.

## Reporting

If you observe discussion that appears to violate this policy, stop it immediately and report it to the Project Lead via private message or email. Do not continue or document the discussion further in project channels.

## Questions

If you are uncertain whether a topic is appropriate, consult your organization's legal counsel before participating.
